Common Questions

Roofing FAQ – The Woodlands & North Houston

Straight answers about roof repair, storm damage, insurance claims, and costs in Texas.

How much does a new roof cost in The Woodlands, TX?

Roof costs depend on size, material, and pitch. In the North Houston area, asphalt shingle roofs typically run $4.50–$6.50 per sq ft and metal roofs $9–$14 per sq ft. Use our free Roof Cost Estimator above for an instant ballpark, or request a free inspection for an exact quote.

Does homeowners insurance cover storm damage roof repair?

In most cases, yes — hail and wind damage are typically covered by Texas homeowners insurance. Storm Pros works directly with your insurance company, documents the damage, and helps you through the entire claim process so you get the full coverage you're entitled to.

How do I know if my roof has storm damage?

Common signs include missing or cracked shingles, dents on vents and flashing, granules in your gutters, water stains on ceilings, and leaks after heavy rain. Some damage is invisible from the ground — our free roof inspection documents everything for your insurance claim.

How long does a roof replacement take?

Most residential roof replacements in The Woodlands area are completed in 1–3 days once materials are on site. Storm Pros schedules fast turnaround after storm events and offers emergency roof repair 24/7.

What areas do you serve?

Storm Pros Roofing & Restoration serves The Woodlands, Conroe, Spring, Tomball, Magnolia, and all of North Houston from our Conroe, TX location at 1601 Primrose St.

Do you offer free roof inspections?

Yes — we offer free, no-obligation roof inspections for homeowners in The Woodlands and North Houston. We assess the damage, document it for your insurance claim, and give you an honest estimate. Emergency inspections are available 24/7.
